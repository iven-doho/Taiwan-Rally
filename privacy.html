This Privacy Policy explains how the Taiwan Tourism Administration San Francisco Office (the "Sponsor," "we," "us") collects, uses, shares and protects information when you visit rally.feeltaiwan.com (the "Site"), play the Rally Around Taiwan game and enter the Taiwan Tourism "Rally Around Taiwan" Sweepstakes (the "Sweepstakes"). It applies to the Site only. Capitalized terms not defined here (such as Entrant, Entry Period, Grand Prize and Gift Prize) have the meaning given in the Official Rules.

1. Who we are

The Site and the Sweepstakes are run by the Taiwan Tourism Administration San Francisco Office. Our agency, DOHO Strategy, operates the Site and the Sweepstakes records on our behalf. You can reach the Feel Taiwan Event Team at info@feeltaiwan.com.

2. What we collect

Information you give us at check-in

  • First and last name, email address and ZIP code (required)
  • Phone number (optional)
  • The airline you would fly to Taiwan (China Airlines, EVA Air or STARLUX Airlines)
  • Your confirmation that you are 18 or older and a US resident, your agreement to the Official Rules, and whether you ticked "Send me exclusive Taiwan travel tips and updates" (optional)

Information about your game

  • Your progress on the board: the spaces and stops you have visited, the questions you have answered and whether your answers were correct, the travel stories you picked, the entries you have earned, and when (date and time)

Information sent automatically with your check-in

  • The campaign tags in the link you used to reach the Site (for example utm_source or utm_campaign) and, if you arrived from a Google ad, the Google Ads click identifier (gclid)
  • Your browser's "user agent" (a short description of your browser, its version and your operating system)

Information collected by analytics and advertising tools

  • Google Analytics and Google Ads collect information such as the pages you view and the game actions you take (for example dice rolls and spaces opened), your device and browser, the site that referred you, and an approximate location derived from your IP address. See Section 5

Information from winners only

  • A shipping address (Gift Prize winners), the information in the Affidavit of Eligibility and releases, identity verification, and the details the issuing airline needs to issue a Grand Prize ticket (for example, name as it appears on the passport)

We do not collect payment information. No purchase is necessary to enter or win.

3. How we use it

  • To run the Sweepstakes: record your entries, prevent duplicate or fraudulent entries, conduct the drawing, contact potential winners, verify eligibility and award prizes
  • To save your game so you can pick up where you left off, on this device or another one, by checking in with the same email address and ZIP code
  • To send you Taiwan travel tips and updates, only if you ticked the optional box
  • To understand how the Site and the game are used, to improve them, and to measure how well our advertising works
  • To comply with law and to protect the Sweepstakes, the Site and its users

4. Where it is stored and who processes it

The check-in information and game progress are stored in a Google Sheet (Google Workspace), written to it by a Google Apps Script web application. The Sheet is kept in a Google account controlled by our agency, DOHO Strategy, which operates it on our behalf. Access is limited to the Sponsor and DOHO Strategy staff who run the Sweepstakes. Google provides these services to us.

Your browser's local storage keeps a copy of your game on your device so the game can continue when you return. See Section 6.

5. Analytics and advertising measurement

The Site uses Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors use the Site and the game, and Google Ads conversion measurement to learn whether our Google ads led people to check in.

Hashed email. When you check in for the first time, the Site sends your email address to Google in hashed form for ad measurement (Google calls this "enhanced conversions"). Before it is sent, the Google tag on the Site converts your email address into a coded string (a SHA-256 "hash"). Google uses the hashed value to match the check-in to its records of Google ad interactions, so that we can count conversions. It is sent once per Entrant; resuming a saved game does not send it again. We do not use it to build advertising audiences.

Google may also collect information about your online activities over time and across different websites when you use the Site. To learn how Google uses this information, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services. You can opt out of Google Analytics with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on and manage the ads Google shows you in My Ad Center.

6. Cookies and local storage

Google Analytics and Google Ads use cookies and similar technologies on the Site. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

The game itself does not use cookies. It saves your game in your browser's local storage on your device. Clearing your browser's site data, or using the game's Reset link, removes that copy from your device; it does not delete your entries or the progress saved to your email address (see Section 9 to ask us to delete them).

Do Not Track. The Site does not currently respond to "Do Not Track" browser signals or to Global Privacy Control signals.

7. How we share information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share it only as follows:

  • Service providers: DOHO Strategy, which operates the Site and the Sweepstakes for us, and Google, which provides Google Workspace, Google Analytics and Google Ads
  • Google, for ad measurement: your hashed email address, as described in Section 5
  • Airlines: China Airlines, EVA Air and STARLUX Airlines receive no personal information about Entrants, except the details of a Grand Prize winner that the issuing airline needs to issue that winner's ticket
  • Winner announcements: winners' names and city/state, as described in the Official Rules
  • Legal reasons: when required by law, or to protect the rights, safety or property of the Sponsor, Entrants or others

8. How long we keep it

  • Check-in information and game progress of Entrants who did not opt in to travel tips: deleted within six (6) months after the drawing
  • Email addresses of Entrants who opted in to travel tips: kept until you unsubscribe
  • Winners' information: kept as long as needed to award the prizes and to meet record-keeping and legal obligations
  • Google Analytics data: kept for 14 months
  • The copy of your game in your browser's local storage: until you clear it

9. Your choices

  • Travel tips: the checkbox at check-in is optional. If you opted in, you can unsubscribe at any time with the link in any of our emails or by emailing us. Opting in or out does not affect your eligibility or your number of entries
  • Access, correction and deletion: email info@feeltaiwan.com with "Rally Around Taiwan privacy request" in the subject line, from the email address you used to check in. We may ask you to confirm the request from that address. If you ask us to delete your information before the drawing, your entries will also be removed
  • Cookies and analytics: see Sections 5 and 6

10. California residents

In the past 12 months we have collected the categories of personal information described in Section 2: identifiers (name, email address, phone number, online identifiers such as the gclid), postal information (ZIP code; shipping address for Gift Prize winners), internet or other electronic network activity (game progress, analytics data), approximate geolocation, and the preferences you give us (airline choice, travel-story picks). We collect it from you, from your browser, and from Google Analytics and Google Ads, for the purposes in Section 3, and disclose it to the recipients in Section 7. We keep it for the periods in Section 8. Apart from the identity documents that potential winners may be asked to show for verification, we do not collect sensitive personal information.

Subject to applicable law, California residents may ask to know what personal information we hold about them, to correct it, and to delete it, and will not be treated differently for making a request. To make a request, use the method in Section 9. An authorized agent may make a request on your behalf with your written permission.

11. Children

The Sweepstakes is open only to US residents 18 years of age or older, and the Site is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it. Entries from persons under 18 are not eligible.

12. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, but no method of transmission over the internet or of electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee its absolute security. Please do not send sensitive information (such as government ID numbers) by email unless we ask for it as part of winner verification.

13. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. We will post the updated version on this page with a new "Last updated" date and, if we make a material change to how we use information already collected, we will tell Entrants by email or on the Site.
