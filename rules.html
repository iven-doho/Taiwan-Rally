1. SWEEPSTAKES PERIOD

The Taiwan Tourism "Rally Around Taiwan" Sweepstakes (the "Sweepstakes") entry period (the "Entry Period") is:

  • Start: November 20, 2026 at 12:00 AM Pacific Time
  • End: December 30, 2026 at 11:59 PM Pacific Time

Entries submitted outside this time period will not be accepted. All times are Pacific Time (PT). The Sponsor's computer is the official timekeeping device for this Sweepstakes.

2. ELIGIBILITY

This Sweepstakes is open only to legal residents of the United States who are 18 years of age or older at the time of entry. Employees, officers, and directors of Taiwan Tourism Administration San Francisco Office (the "Sponsor"), China Airlines, EVA Air and STARLUX Airlines (the "Prize Providers"), and their respective parent companies, subsidiaries, affiliates, advertising and promotion agencies, and the immediate family members (spouse, parents, children, and siblings and their spouses) and household members of each are not eligible to enter or win.

This Sweepstakes is conducted entirely online and no physical presence is required. This Sweepstakes is void where prohibited by law.

3. HOW TO ENTER

To Enter:

  1. Visit the official sweepstakes site at rally.feeltaiwan.com (the "Site") during the Entry Period
  2. Complete the check-in form with your legal first name, last name, valid email address, and ZIP code (a phone number is optional)
  3. Choose the airline you would fly to Taiwan: China Airlines, EVA Air or STARLUX Airlines. Your choice does not affect your odds of winning and does not determine which airline's ticket a Grand Prize winner receives
  4. Agree to the Official Rules and confirm you are 18 years of age or older and a US resident
  5. Submit your entry to receive one (1) base sweepstakes entry

Each person who submits a complete check-in form during the Entry Period is an "Entrant."

Bonus Entries (Optional):

After checking in, Entrants may earn up to fourteen (14) additional entries by playing the free "Rally Around Taiwan" online board game on the Site:

  • +1 entry for each of the twelve (12) destination spaces explored. A space is explored when all three of its questions have been answered. Answers do not need to be correct on the first try; an Entrant may try again
  • +1 entry for the stop at the Taiwan PASS space
  • +1 entry for completing all fourteen (14) stops on the board (the check-in at the airport, the twelve destination spaces and the Taiwan PASS space)
  • Maximum of fifteen (15) total entries per person (1 base entry + up to 14 bonus entries)
  • Each bonus entry may be earned only once per Entrant
  • Playing the game is optional and free; no purchase or payment is required to play
  • Game progress is saved to the Entrant's email address. An Entrant may resume on any device by checking in again with the same email address and ZIP code; checking in again does not create an additional base entry
  • Bonus entries must be earned during the Entry Period
  • Sponsor reserves the right to verify game progress

Entry Restrictions:

  • Limit one (1) registration per person/email address
  • Multiple registrations from the same person using different email addresses are prohibited and may result in disqualification
  • All entry information must be accurate and complete
  • Entrants must use their legal name as it appears on their government-issued identification
  • Use of automated entry devices, bots, or programs is prohibited
  • Entries that are incomplete, illegible, corrupted, or received after the Entry Period will be void

The Grand Prize tickets are provided by China Airlines, EVA Air and STARLUX Airlines, one (1) ticket from each airline. Which Grand Prize winner receives which airline's ticket is at Sponsor's discretion.

Approximate Retail Value (ARV): $1,200 USD per Grand Prize

5. WINNER SELECTION & NOTIFICATION

Random Drawing:

  • Three (3) Grand Prize winners will be selected in a random drawing from all eligible entries received during the Entry Period
  • Gift Prize winners will then be selected in a random drawing from the remaining eligible entries; entries of Grand Prize winners, and of Entrants already selected for a Gift Prize, are excluded
  • Drawing will be held on or around December 31, 2026
  • Drawing will be conducted by Taiwan Tourism Administration San Francisco Office or its designated representatives
  • Odds of winning depend on the number of eligible entries received and on the number of entries each Entrant earns

Winner Notification:

  • Winners will be announced in early January 2027 on the Sweepstakes website, rally.feeltaiwan.com, and on Taiwan Tourism North America's official social media accounts, by first name, last initial, and city and state
  • Potential winners will be notified by email at the address provided at registration within five (5) business days of the drawing
  • Potential winners may be required to sign and return an Affidavit of Eligibility, Liability Release, and (where legal) Publicity Release within seven (7) days of prize notification
  • Potential Gift Prize winners will be asked for a shipping address
  • Failure to comply may result in forfeiture and selection of an alternate winner

Verification:

  • All potential winners are subject to verification of eligibility and compliance with these Official Rules
  • Winner must present valid government-issued photo identification matching the name provided at registration
  • Sponsor reserves the right to disqualify any entrant if fraud or rule violations are suspected
