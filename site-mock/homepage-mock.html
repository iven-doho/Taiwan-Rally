How Round Taiwan Rally Works

A dice-and-passport journey around the real Taiwan — on the Tourism Administration's official island map.

1

Check in — you're entered

Register with your name and email and your first sweepstakes entry is locked in. Pick your travel token: OhBear, the Taiwan logo, or a partner airline plane.

2

Roll & explore the island

Fly into Taoyuan, then roll the die and travel clockwise around Taiwan — 33 real destinations, from Taipei 101 to Kenting, plus scenic shuttle detours into the mountains. Answer one quick question to stamp each into your Taiwan Passport.

3

Milestones = bonus entries

Finish laps, complete regions, earn your Taiwan PASS on the rails, collect the whole island — up to 13 total entries in the flight ticket draw.

Play as little or as much as you like — your entry stands either way, and your passport saves for whenever you return.
